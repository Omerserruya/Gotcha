# GOTCHA Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the agreement between GOTCHA, the operator of gotcha.co.il ("GOTCHA", the "Processor"), and the customer that has an account on the GOTCHA platform (the "Customer", the "Controller"), governing use of the GOTCHA platform (the "Service") under the Terms of Service at ./terms-of-service.md (the "Agreement").

This DPA applies to the extent GOTCHA processes personal data on the Customer's behalf in providing the Service. It is effective as of the date the Customer accepts the Agreement or starts using the Service, whichever is earlier.

## 1. Definitions

- "Personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach", and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") where it applies and Israel's Protection of Privacy Law where it applies.
- "Customer Personal Data" means personal data contained in Customer Data (as defined in the Agreement) that GOTCHA processes on the Customer's behalf.
- "Subprocessor" means a third party engaged by GOTCHA to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles of the Parties

- For Customer Personal Data (the personal data of the Customer's end customers and contacts processed through the Service), the Customer is the controller and GOTCHA is the processor.
- For platform account data (the Customer's staff users, waitlist entries, and billing contacts), GOTCHA is the controller; that processing is described in the Privacy Policy at ./privacy-policy.md and is outside the scope of this DPA.
- The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness of the Customer Personal Data it processes through the Service, including having a lawful basis and providing any notices or obtaining any consents required of a controller.

## 3. Subject Matter, Duration, Nature, and Purpose

- **Subject matter.** Provision of the GOTCHA platform: businesses connect their messaging channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, email, Slack, voice) and GOTCHA provides a shared team inbox, AI assistants, knowledge-base answers, and CRM/commerce integrations.
- **Duration.** The term of the Agreement, plus the deletion period described in Section 10.
- **Nature of processing.** Hosting, storage, transmission, retrieval, analysis, and AI-assisted generation of replies and summaries; indexing of knowledge-base content including creation of text embeddings; handling of voice calls and call analysis where the Customer enables the voice channel; routine backup and restoration.
- **Purpose.** To provide, secure, and maintain the Service for the Customer as described in the Agreement and the Customer's configuration.

## 4. Categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data

**Data subjects:**

- The Customer's end customers, contacts, and leads who communicate with the Customer through connected channels.
- Participants in voice calls handled through the Customer's voice channel, where enabled.
- Individuals whose data appears in content the Customer uploads to the knowledge base or syncs from connected systems.

**Categories of personal data:**

- Contact and identity data: names, phone numbers, email addresses, channel identifiers, avatars, tags, and custom fields the Customer records.
- Communication content: messages sent and received through connected channels, including attachments and media, and conversation metadata.
- AI-derived data: conversation summaries, detected intents and sentiment, extracted facts about customers, and behavioral briefs generated by the Service.
- Voice data, where the Customer enables the voice channel: caller phone numbers, call metadata, call analysis summaries and transcript-derived frames, and references to call recordings held by the Customer's telephony provider.
- Consent and preference records the Customer maintains in the Service, including opt-out status.
- Data from systems the Customer connects (CRM, commerce, calendar, knowledge sources), to the extent the Customer instructs the Service to read or write it.

The Service is not intended for special categories of personal data. The Customer should not direct such data into the Service; if end customers volunteer it in message content, it is processed as communication content under this DPA.

## 5. Documented Instructions

- GOTCHA will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to transfers, unless required to do otherwise by law that applies to GOTCHA; in that case GOTCHA will inform the Customer of the legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits it.
- The Customer's instructions are: the Agreement, this DPA, the Customer's configuration of the Service (including connected channels and integrations, AI assistant settings, retention policies, and use of export and erasure tools), and any additional written instructions the parties agree.
- GOTCHA will inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data protection law. GOTCHA may suspend performance of such an instruction until it is confirmed or modified.

## 6. Confidentiality

GOTCHA ensures that persons it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations, contractual or statutory, and process the data only as needed to provide the Service.

## 7. Security Measures (Annex-Style Description)

Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, GOTCHA implements the following technical and organizational measures:

**Access control and authentication**

- Sign-in through a self-hosted identity service using OIDC single sign-on; the GOTCHA application stores no passwords.
- Multi-factor authentication support, including per-tenant enforcement policies; platform administrators are always required to use MFA.
- Role-based access control within each workspace, with administrator-gated management of users and permissions.
- The authentication gate fails closed: if identity cannot be verified, access is denied rather than allowed.

**Tenant isolation**

- The platform is multi-tenant; tenant isolation is enforced at the data layer, and requests are scoped to the authenticated tenant.

**Encryption**

- TLS encryption for data in transit at the network edge; inbound traffic reaches the platform through an outbound-only network tunnel with no directly exposed inbound ports.
- Stored credentials and secrets for connected channels and integrations are encrypted at the field level with AES-256-GCM.

**Logging and monitoring**

- Audit logging of security-relevant and data-lifecycle actions, including user lifecycle changes, permission changes, consent changes, and data subject request completion, scoped per tenant.
- AI usage is metered and logged (token counts and cost metadata; the usage log does not store prompt or response text).

**Availability and backups**

- Single-region hosting on infrastructure GOTCHA manages in AWS il-central-1 (Israel).
- Daily backups: nightly database dumps, encrypted in transit, stored in S3 in the same account, with a lifecycle that transitions backups at 30 days and deletes them at 90 days; daily infrastructure snapshots are kept for 7 days.

**Data lifecycle**

- Built-in export and erasure endpoints for users and end-customer contacts; erasure is a hard delete of the affected records, including purge of associated knowledge-base vectors.
- Per-tenant configurable retention policies for messages, AI usage logs, and audit logs.

The Customer is responsible for the security measures on its side, including safeguarding its users' credentials, configuring roles and MFA appropriately, and securing the third-party accounts it connects.

## 8. Subprocessors

- The Customer gives GOTCHA general authorization to engage Subprocessors for the processing described in this DPA.
- The current list of Subprocessors, including purpose, data, and region for each, is published at ./subprocessors.md. That page also distinguishes Subprocessors from tenant-controlled integrations, which activate only when the Customer connects them and which act on the Customer's instruction rather than as GOTCHA's Subprocessors.
- GOTCHA will update that list before adding or replacing a Subprocessor. Customers can request notification of changes by emailing privacy@gotcha.co.il.
- If the Customer has a reasonable, data-protection-related objection to a new Subprocessor, the parties will discuss it in good faith. If no resolution is found, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the Service.
- GOTCHA imposes data protection obligations on its Subprocessors that are materially no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains responsible to the Customer for its Subprocessors' performance.
- Self-hosted components operated by GOTCHA on its own infrastructure (the identity service, the vector database, the relational database, and the cache/queue layer) are part of the Service, not Subprocessors.

## 9. Assistance with Data Subject Requests

- Taking into account the nature of the processing, GOTCHA assists the Customer, through appropriate technical and organizational measures, in fulfilling the Customer's obligation to respond to data subject requests.
- The Service provides built-in, administrator-operated tools for this: export endpoints for users and end-customer contacts (access and portability, returned in a structured JSON format), erasure endpoints that hard-delete the subject's records including associated vectors, and consent records.
- There is no self-service portal for data subjects; requests are fulfilled through the Customer's administrators or, where needed, with GOTCHA's support via privacy@gotcha.co.il. Identity verification of requesters is performed manually.
- If a data subject contacts GOTCHA directly about Customer Personal Data, GOTCHA will redirect the request to the Customer without undue delay and will not respond substantively except on the Customer's instruction or where legally required.
- GOTCHA will provide reasonable assistance with the Customer's obligations regarding security, breach notification, and data protection impact assessments, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to GOTCHA.

## 10. Deletion and Return at End of Provision

- During the term, the Customer can export Customer Personal Data at any time using the built-in export tools.
- At termination of the Agreement, GOTCHA will, at the Customer's choice, enable a final export and then delete Customer Personal Data using the platform's erasure mechanisms, which permanently delete the data from production systems.
- Copies in backups are not immediately erased; they expire through the backup lifecycle, so deleted production data leaves backups within at most 90 days of deletion.
- GOTCHA may retain data to the extent required by law, and only for as long as and to the extent so required.

## 11. Personal Data Breach Notification

- GOTCHA commits to notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware where feasible.
- The notification will describe, to the extent then known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects. Information may be provided in phases as it becomes available.
- GOTCHA will reasonably cooperate with the Customer's own notification obligations to supervisory authorities and data subjects. Notification is not an admission of fault.

## 12. Audits

- GOTCHA will make available to the Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, on request to privacy@gotcha.co.il.
- Where the information provided is not reasonably sufficient, the Customer may conduct, or mandate an independent auditor bound by confidentiality to conduct, an audit limited to GOTCHA's processing of Customer Personal Data.
- Audits require reasonable prior written notice, take place at a reasonable frequency (no more than once per year, absent a supervisory authority requirement or a personal data breach), occur during business hours, must not unreasonably disrupt operations, and must not give access to other customers' data. Each party bears its own audit costs.

## 13. International Transfers

- Customer Personal Data is hosted and primarily processed in Israel (AWS region il-central-1). Israel benefits from a European Commission adequacy decision, so transfers of personal data from the EEA to Israel may rely on that decision.
- Some Subprocessors process data outside Israel. In particular, AI text generation and embeddings are performed by OpenAI in the United States, and, where the Customer enables an optional speech-to-text provider for its voice channel, call audio may be processed in the United States (Deepgram) or per the configured Google Cloud region. For these transfers, GOTCHA relies on the respective provider's data processing terms, including the transfer safeguards those terms incorporate.
- The regions of all Subprocessors are listed at ./subprocessors.md.
- GOTCHA will not transfer Customer Personal Data to a country without appropriate safeguards recognized by applicable data protection law.

## 14. Liability and Order of Precedence

- Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Agreement, except where applicable data protection law does not permit such limitation.
- In case of conflict between this DPA and the Agreement regarding the processing of personal data, this DPA prevails.

## 15. Term and Changes

- This DPA remains in force for as long as GOTCHA processes Customer Personal Data on the Customer's behalf.
- GOTCHA may update this DPA to reflect changes in law or in the Service; material changes will be notified as described in the Agreement. The version published at the time of processing applies.
