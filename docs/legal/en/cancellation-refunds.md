# GOTCHA Cancellation and Refund Policy

> This English text is a translation provided for convenience. The Hebrew version of this document is the authoritative one, and in case of any conflict or inconsistency between the versions, the Hebrew version prevails.

Effective date: August 3, 2026

This policy governs cancellation, renewal, refunds, and the use of GOTCHA services. Using GOTCHA services constitutes acceptance of this policy, in addition to the company's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to business customers using GOTCHA services (B2B).

Where a commercial agreement, service agreement, or custom agreement has been signed between GOTCHA and a customer, the terms of the written agreement prevail over this policy in the event of a conflict.

## 2. Subscription plans and automatic renewal

GOTCHA offers monthly subscription plans and annual subscription plans paid in advance.

Every subscription renews automatically at the end of the relevant billing period, unless the customer cancelled the subscription before the renewal date.

Cancelling a subscription prevents future charges only. It does not entitle the customer to a refund for a billing period already paid for.

## 3. Cancelling a subscription

Customers can cancel their subscription through the account settings in the GOTCHA system.

Upon cancellation:

- The subscription remains active until the end of the period paid for.
- No further charges are made after the end of that period.
- The customer may continue using the service under the active plan until the period ends.

Cancelling a subscription does not immediately delete the account or its data. Data handling after the service ends is carried out in accordance with GOTCHA's Data Retention Policy.

## 4. Refunds

Payments for GOTCHA services are not automatically refundable.

Refunds are granted only in cases such as:

- An incorrect charge.
- A duplicate charge.
- A significant technical fault caused by GOTCHA.
- Other exceptional cases, at GOTCHA's discretion.

GOTCHA reserves the right to approve or decline refund requests according to the circumstances of the case.

Where a refund is approved, it is issued within up to 30 days from the date of approval, to the original payment method, subject to the limitations of the payment providers.

## 5. Trial period

Where a trial period is offered, it lasts 14 days, unless stated otherwise at sign-up.

The trial period requires a valid payment method.

At the end of the trial period, the account automatically moves to a paid plan according to the plan selected at sign-up, unless the customer cancelled the subscription before the trial period ended.

## 6. AI Credits

Some GOTCHA plans include an allocation of AI Credits for use with the system's artificial intelligence services.

AI Credits:

- Are replenished at the start of each billing period.
- Do not accumulate across billing periods.
- Any balance not used by the end of the billing period expires.

Where a customer has reached their credit limit, GOTCHA may allow the purchase of an additional credit package, or the activation of a credit replenishment mechanism, according to the options available in the account.

Credits purchased in addition to the subscription (Top-up Credits):

- Are valid only during the billing period in which they were purchased.
- Do not carry over to future billing periods.
- Are not convertible to money and are not refundable.

If a subscription is cancelled, the customer may continue to use the existing credit balance until the end of the billing period already paid for. When that period ends, any unused credit balance expires.

## 7. Suspension or termination of service by GOTCHA

GOTCHA may suspend or terminate access to the service in the event of:

- A breach of the Terms of Service.
- Unlawful or unauthorised use of the service.
- An attempt to harm the system, its security, or other users.
- Activity suspected of being fraudulent or abusive.
- Any action GOTCHA reasonably believes may endanger the service, the company, or other users.

In these cases GOTCHA may restrict or close the account according to the circumstances, and the customer is not entitled to a refund for periods already paid for, unless GOTCHA decides otherwise at its discretion.

## 8. Price changes

GOTCHA may update service prices from time to time.

Price changes apply only from the billing period following appropriate notice to the customer.

Continued use of the service after a price change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated price.

## 9. Taxes and payments

Prices displayed by GOTCHA do not include taxes, levies, or governmental charges, unless expressly stated otherwise.

The customer is responsible for any tax applicable to them under the relevant law.

## 10. Data after the service ends

After a subscription ends, the customer's access to the service may be restricted or blocked.

Customer data is retained, processed, or deleted in accordance with GOTCHA's Data Retention Policy and applicable law.

It is the customer's responsibility to export any information they need before their access to the service ends.

## 11. Exceptions and discretion

GOTCHA may, at its sole discretion, approve exceptions to this policy in special cases.

Granting a refund, a credit, or an exception in a particular case does not constitute an undertaking or a precedent for future cases.

## 12. Contact

For questions, cancellation requests, or refund enquiries, you can contact the GOTCHA team using the contact details published on the company's website.
