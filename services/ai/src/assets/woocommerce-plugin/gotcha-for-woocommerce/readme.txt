Your GOTCHA AI employee answers your customers on your store, with your products and orders.

== Description ==

Connect your WooCommerce store to your GOTCHA workspace in one click. The GOTCHA chat appears on your store's pages; your AI employee answers your customers there, knows your products and their orders, and hands over to your team when needed.

Every setting of the chat (its look, its welcome, who answers) lives in GOTCHA. This plugin connects the store and puts the chat on its pages.

== Installation ==

1. In WP Admin, Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the zip you downloaded from GOTCHA, and activate it.
2. Go to WooCommerce → GOTCHA and press "Connect to GOTCHA".
3. Sign in to GOTCHA and approve the connection. You are brought back here, connected.

== Privacy ==

The chat loads from GOTCHA's servers on your store's pages. When a cookie / consent plugin that supports the WP Consent API is active, the chat waits for the visitor's consent to functional cookies.
